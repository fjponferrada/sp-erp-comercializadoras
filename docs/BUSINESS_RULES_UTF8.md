## 9. CCH and Invoice Parsing Rules

### Rule 169: CCH Timezones and Inclusive Periods

### Rule 170: F1 Date Shifting
When importing F1 files, the `FechaDesdeFactura` and `FechaHastaFactura` represent the dates at which the reading was taken (e.g., "a cierre de 31 de mayo"). Therefore, the **ACTUAL** billing period starts on the NEXT day (e.g., June 1) and ends on the end date itself. We MUST shift `fechaInicio` and `fechaFin` forward by 1 day when saving them to the database, so they represent the TRUE inclusive start and end dates of the billing period in local time. For example, "31/5 a 30/6" in F1 should be stored and displayed as "1/6 a 30/6".
